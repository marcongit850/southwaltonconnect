Project documents & sources

Review the original records.

This page provides access to key official documents referenced throughout the South Walton Connector information site, including materials from Walton County, regional transportation planning organizations, and the project PD&E team.

The documents here are collected for community information. This website is an independent community resource and is not an official Walton County website. Copies below are the public files from Walton County's document center that match the records listed on this site. The large display boards stay on the county site and are linked where a local copy would be unwieldy.

Official source document

2019 Public Meeting Materials — August 21, 2019

Walton County | August 21, 2019

The public meeting handout from the first formal public session, when six build alternatives—A, B, C1, C2, D1 and D2—were presented. The large alternatives display board, with the maps, is linked from the county document center.

Official source document

D2 Preferred Alternative / Public Meeting Materials — April 18, 2023

Walton County / project team | April 18, 2023

Public meeting handout identifying Alternative D2 as the preferred route, a new two-lane north-south corridor between CR 30A and US 98. The evaluation matrix from that meeting compares the alternatives.

Download the handout Download the evaluation matrix

Official source document

Proposed Roadway Typical Section

Project team | August 21, 2019 typical sections; restated in 2023

Technical cross-sections for the alternatives, including the D2 concept: 11-foot travel lanes and a 12-foot multi-use path within about 150 feet of right-of-way. The April 18, 2023 handout describes that same D2 section.
