About our sources

Project Documents & Sources

The documents provided here are collected for community informational purposes. While every effort is made to provide the most current official records, this website is an independent community resource and is not an official government platform. The summaries and interpretations provided elsewhere on this site should not be treated as a substitute for reviewing the original source recordings in their entirety.

Review the original records.

This page provides access to key official documents referenced throughout the South Walton Connector information site, including materials from Walton County, regional transportation planning organizations, and the project PD&E team. Keeping community members informed with direct access to source records is a priority for this independent information resource.
