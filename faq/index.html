Frequently Asked Questions

What is the South Walton Connector?

The South Walton Connector is a proposed additional public connection between Scenic 30A and US 98 in Walton County, intended to manage regional transportation and emergency access.

What is D2?

D2 refers to one of the proposed corridors for the connector route that would traverse portions of the Point Washington State Forest.

Has the road been approved for construction?

No. At this time, the project is a proposal and has not received official approval for construction or secured necessary funding.

Why was another 30A–US 98 connection studied?

The potential connection was studied to evaluate its impact on persistent traffic congestion, emergency response effectiveness, hurricane evacuation routes, and environmental preservation.

Why are people concerned about Point Washington State Forest?

Concerns center on the ecological impact of a road corridor through state forest land, potential habitat fragmentation, and the possibility of future development pressure near the roadway.

Could development along the road be prohibited?

One area of investigation is whether permanent protections—such as prohibiting new private driveways and commercial entrances—could be implemented to safeguard the forest portion of the corridor.
