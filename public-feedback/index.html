Have Your Say on the South Walton Connector

South Walton residents, property owners, businesses and visitors are invited to share their views about a potential additional connection between Scenic 30A and US 98.

TAKE THE COMMUNITY SURVEY BELOW

What best describes your connection to South Walton? Select all that apply.
What area of South Walton are you most closely associated with?
How serious do you believe traffic congestion on Scenic Highway 30A is?
Are you aware that the nearby route on Watersound Parkway is a private road and is not a Walton County-maintained public connector road between Scenic 30A and US 98?
Do you believe South Walton needs an additional public north-south connection between Scenic Highway 30A and US 98?
Which transportation issues are most important to you? Select up to three.
Based on what you currently know, what is your opinion of the proposed D2 corridor through Point Washington State Forest?
If a connector were constructed through Point Washington State Forest, should permanent, legally enforceable protections be required to prohibit commercial and residential development along the State Forest portion of the corridor?
If permanent, legally enforceable protections prohibited commercial and residential development along the State Forest portion of the connector, how would that affect your opinion of D2?
If future commercial and residential development along the State Forest portion of the corridor were permanently prohibited, would you support construction of the D2 connector?
If the State Forest portion of the connector were permanently limited-access with no commercial entrances, residential driveways, subdivision entrances, or new private connecting streets, how would that affect your opinion of D2?
Which statement comes closest to your current view?

About this survey: This is an independent community survey and is not an official Walton County survey. Participation is voluntary. Results reflect the people who choose to participate and should not be interpreted as a scientific or statistically representative poll of Walton County residents.
